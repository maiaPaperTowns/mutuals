**Pre:** review your event matches, explain shared interests and save favorites.

**Post:** review completed connections, consult the internal Pre/During roles, and prepare and save private follow-up drafts using your allowed channels. Pending/declined connections cannot receive a follow-up draft. You copy and send drafts yourself.

**During:** open the [mutuals website](https://mhacks-live-map.vercel.app/events) for live networking and GPS. This Agent does not fabricate your location.

## Connect your existing account

Sign in on the website, save your profile and join an event. In **Use mutuals in ASI:One**, generate a code, then send `link <code>` in this chat. Codes expire after five minutes. Each chat needs its own authorization, which expires after 24 hours. Linking another chat replaces this one. Send `unlink` or disconnect on the website to revoke access.

Send `events` to list your own events and `event <id>` to choose one. Then ask `Who should I meet and why?` in Pre, or `recap` and `Prepare and save a follow-up draft for <completed connection>` in Post. All results use your existing mutuals account.

This is one ACP Agent with native internal assistant roles. Mailbox provides delivery; our running host executes requests. Private access is disabled until live ASI routing and session isolation are verified. No successful link/draft is claimed while that gate is pending.
